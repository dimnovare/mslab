2a Framed atelier

Built from the logo itself: the thin rectangular frame and the tracked capitals of "KOOLITUSKESKUS" become the layout system. Square corners, 1px charcoal rules, Orchid Tint surfaces, and courses shown as a numbered index instead of cards. It feels more like a studio or a printed programme than a website. The rest of the palette and type stay the same as 1a.
